*Profile*  
Prof. Peter N. Mwita is a distinguished Kenyan academic leader and statistician with over 30 years’ experience in higher education, research, and national development. As Vice-Chancellor of Kaimosi Friends University, he leads a transformative agenda anchored on academic excellence, research expansion, fiscal discipline, strong governance, and community-centered growth.

*Academic Background*  
1. *PhD, Statistics* – Technische Universität Kaiserslautern, Germany  
2. *MSc, Applied Statistics* – Macquarie University, Australia  
3. *BSc, Statistics – First Class Honours* – Kenyatta University

*Research & Academic Leadership*
